Transport Tracking — Customer Retained Pallet Tracking ?Ask any doubt

This section is Transport's own count — it mainly tells you which pallets are still retained with customers (not yet collected), by day. It is not a full mirror of the warehouse ledger: Transport reports what's outstanding, not necessarily every movement the warehouse tracks. Use the Dispatched/Returned columns below as a cross-check against the warehouse figures, not as a strict match requirement — a difference there is informational, not automatically an error.
Filtering here also filters the Aujan Pallet Inbound & Outbound Tracker below to the same dates, so picking e.g. 20 Aug shows both sides — Transport's count and the warehouse ledger — for that same day, side by side.
