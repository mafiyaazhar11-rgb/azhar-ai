📦 Order Value Tier — ≥ AED 1,000 vs < AED 1,000

Orders and drops split at AED 1,000, by BU and by temperature (Ambient/Frozen — the two can't share a truck, so they're never combined into one drop). Combined means: multiple orders sharing the same (date, route, location) — the same truck's stop at that address that day — ride together and have their values SUMMED before checking the AED 1,000 line, so e.g. AED 500 + 200 + 300 on one truck's stop counts as one AED 1,000 drop, not three small orders judged individually. This matches the drop definition already validated in Transport Cost Reconciliation.
